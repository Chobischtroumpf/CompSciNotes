> [!info]+ Definition
> [[ULB - Sciences Informatiques/BA2/INFO-H303/Chapters/1 - Database Management Systems/Data#^493a3b|Data]] that continues to exist even when the system is not active. It must be stored in secondary memory (not just in computer memory) and organized to be made available to several applications.

> [!tip]
> In practice, information systems **often** require **persistent data**.
