>[!info]+ Definition
> A **table** is a structured collection of related [[ULB - Sciences Informatiques/BA2/INFO-H303/Chapters/1 - Database Management Systems/Data#^493a3b|data]] organized into **rows (records)** and **columns (attributes)** within a [[Database#^fedb9b|database]]. Each table represents an entity (e.g., `Student`, `Course`) and stores multiple instances of that entity.
