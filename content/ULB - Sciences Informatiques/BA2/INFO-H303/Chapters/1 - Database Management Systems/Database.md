> [!info]+ Definition
> A **database** is a structured collection of related [[ULB - Sciences Informatiques/BA2/INFO-H303/Chapters/1 - Database Management Systems/Data#^493a3b|data]].

^fedb9b

It possesses:
- **Logical coherence** - Can be characterized as a whole.
- **Inherent meaning** - Represents a partial view of the real world.
- **A specific purpose** - Designed for an intended group of users and applications, embodying an operational rather than philosophical view.
- **Varying size** - Ranges from small personal records to large-scale national databases.
- **Broad or narrow scope** - Can contain anything from simple address lists to comprehensive multimedia encyclopedias.
- **Physical organization of varying complexity** - From simple files to distributed multi-user databases.
- **Logically coordinated objectives** - Defined once for a community of users but accessed by various applications with specific needs.
