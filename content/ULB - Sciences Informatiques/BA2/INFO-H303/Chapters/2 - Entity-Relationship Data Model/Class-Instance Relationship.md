> [!info]+ Definition
> The **class-instance relationship** describes the connection between a **class (type)** and its **instances (objects)**. This relationship is known as **instantiation** or **classification**.
> - A **class** serves as a **template** that defines **attributes** (e.g., `Name`, `BDate`, `Job` in the `Person` class).
> - An **instance** is a **specific occurrence** of a class, having concrete values for each defined attribute (e.g., `John` and `Peter` are instances of `Person`).
