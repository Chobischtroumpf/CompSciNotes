> [!info]+ Definition
> A **constraint** is a rule enforced on [[Attribute#^eca05e|attributes]] or [[Table#^a376c8|tables]] to ensure **data integrity and validity**. Common constraints include:
> - **Primary Key (PK)** – Ensures uniqueness of a record (e.g., `StudentNo`).
> - **Foreign Key (FK)** – Enforces referential integrity between [[Table#^a376c8|tables]].
> - **NOT NULL** – Prevents empty values in a column.
> - **CHECK** – Enforces specific conditions (e.g., `Grade BETWEEN 0 AND 20`).
> - **UNIQUE** – Ensures no duplicate values in an [[Attribute#^eca05e|attribute]].
