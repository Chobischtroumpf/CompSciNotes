> [!info]+ Definition
> **Relation Schema**: $R(A_1 : D_1, \ldots, A_n : D_n)$
> - $R$ = name of the relation
> - Domains $(D_1, \ldots, D_n)$ = sets of atomic values; domains in $R$ are not necessarily all distinct
> - Attributes $A_1, \ldots, A_n$, all distinct in $R$
> - Structure $(A_1 : D_1, \ldots, A_n : D_n)$ (i.e., the data type of relation tuples)
