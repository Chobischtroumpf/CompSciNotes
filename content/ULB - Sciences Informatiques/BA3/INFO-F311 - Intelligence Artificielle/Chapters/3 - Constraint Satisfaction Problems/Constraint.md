> [!info]+ Definition
> **Constraints** are restrictions that specify allowable combinations of values for variables in a CSP.

## Types of Constraints

> [!abstract]+ Constraint Classification
> **Unary constraints:**
> - Involve a single variable
> - Example: $SA \neq green$
>
> **Binary constraints:**
> - Involve pairs of variables
> - Example: $SA \neq WA$
>
> **Higher-order constraints:**
> - Involve 3+ variables
> - Example: Cryptarithmetic column constraints
>
> **Soft constraints (preferences):**
> - Represent desirable but not required properties
> - Used in optimization problems
