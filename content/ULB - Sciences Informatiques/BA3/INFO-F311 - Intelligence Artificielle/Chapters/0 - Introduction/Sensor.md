> [!info]+ Definition
> A **sensor** is a component that allows an agent to perceive and gather information from its environment.

> [!example]+ Examples
> - Cameras
> - Microphones
> - Keyboards
> - GPS
> - Temperature sensors

> [!note]+ Related Concepts
> - **[[Agent]]**: Entity using sensors
> - **[[Actuator]]**: Counterpart for acting
> - **[[Reflex Agent]]**: Uses sensors for immediate decisions
