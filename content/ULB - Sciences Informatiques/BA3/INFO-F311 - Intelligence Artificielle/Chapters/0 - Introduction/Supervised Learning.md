> [!info]+ Definition
> **Supervised learning** is learning to predict outputs from inputs by training on labeled examples that show correct input-output pairs.

> [!question]+ Core Question
> How can I learn a model of the world from data?
>
> **Intelligence from Data/Experience**

> - **[[Reinforcement Learning (Intro)]]**: Learning through interaction
