> [!info]+ Definition
> **Access networks** are physical media with wired/wireless [[Communication link#^b83bd9|communication links]] that connect end systems to edge routers.

> [!abstract]- Types of Access [[Network#^89dac9|Networks]]
> **Residential access networks**:
> - DSL (Digital Subscriber Line) access
> - Cable-based access
>
> **Institutional access networks**:
> - Schools and companies
> - Enterprise networks
>
> **Mobile access networks**:
> - WiFi (wireless local area)
> - 4G/5G (cellular wide area)

> [!note]+ Characteristics
> - **Transmission rate**: Bandwidth available (bits per second)
> - **Access type**: Shared vs dedicated access among users
> - **Coverage area**: Local (WiFi) vs wide area (cellular)

> [!example]+ Access Network Comparison
>
> |Type|Speed|Sharing|Use Case|
> |---|---|---|---|
> |DSL|Up to 100 Mbps|Dedicated|Home broadband|
> |Cable|Up to 1 Gbps|Shared neighborhood|Home/business|
> |Fiber|Up to 10 Gbps|Dedicated|High-speed broadband|
> |WiFi|Up to 1 Gbps|Shared local|Indoor wireless|
> |4G/5G|Up to 1 Gbps|Shared cell|Mobile connectivity|
