> [!info]+ Definition
> A **protocol** is a set of rules and standards that governs how devices communicate over a [[Network#^89dac9|network]], defining the format, timing, sequencing, and error handling for sending/receiving messages.

^4bfd4c

> [!abstract]- Protocol Specifications
> Protocols define:
> - **Message format**: Structure and content of messages
> - **Message order**: Sequence in which messages are sent/received
> - **Actions taken**: What to do when messages are transmitted/received
> - **Error handling**: How to respond to unexpected events

> [!example]+ Common Internet Protocols
> - **HTTP** (Web)
> - **SMTP** (email)
> - **TCP** (reliable transport)
> - **IP** (network routing)
> - **WiFi** (wireless link)
> - **4G** (cellular)
> - **Ethernet** (wired link)
