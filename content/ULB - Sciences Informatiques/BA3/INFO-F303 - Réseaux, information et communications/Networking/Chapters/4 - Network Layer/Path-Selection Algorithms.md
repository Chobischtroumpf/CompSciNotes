> [!info]+ **Path-selection algorithms**
> **Path-selection algorithms** determine the routes that packets take through the network. These algorithms are implemented in:
>
> - Routing protocols (OSPF, BGP)
> - SDN controller

^02a2bb

> [!tip]+
> Path-selection algorithms operate at the control plane level and compute the forwarding tables used by routers.
