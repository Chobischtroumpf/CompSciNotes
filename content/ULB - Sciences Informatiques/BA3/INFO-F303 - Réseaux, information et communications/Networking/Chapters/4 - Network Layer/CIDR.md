> [!note] CIDR
> CIDR stands for Classless InterDomain Routing
>
> Its key features, and improvement on the previous [[Classful addresses]] are:
> - The subnet portion of the address can be of arbitrary length
> - The address is now formatted like this:
> 	- a.b.c.d/x, where x is the number of bits in the subnet portion of the address
