> [!info]+ Définition The **ICMP protocol** (Internet Control Message Protocol) provides:
>
> - Error reporting capabilities
> - Router "signaling" functions

> [!tip]+ To Note:
> ICMP allows routers and hosts to communicate network-layer information, such as error conditions and diagnostic information (e.g., ping, traceroute).
